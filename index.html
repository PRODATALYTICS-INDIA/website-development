Services

What we do

From first conversation to deployed solution, we bridge complex analytics and practical business intelligence — with senior, hands-on involvement at every step.

Data science consulting

Strategic data initiatives that align with your business objectives. We sit one to one with clients to understand their pain points and figure out solutions. We also help validating the methodology or approach they have made to build more confidence.

Product development

From Machine learning models to complex Computer vision challenges and statistical calculations, we develop and deliver high-end scalable products—APIs, dashboards, forms, or other modes—tailored to requirements to create market presence.

Training and internship program

Comprehensive training programs and hands-on 12-week internship programs to build data science capabilities and real-world experience.

Statistical reporting

Precise statistical reporting and consultations for confident decision-making. We also help produce automated reports and support students, research scholars, and other professionals to understand and apply right statistical techniques, and effectively generate interpretations with visuals and usage of proper statistical methods.

Workshops, masterclass & collaboration with academia

Hands-on workshops and partnerships with universities to foster innovation. Not only with universities but with institutions and industry, we architect courses and workshop sessions to enhance their productivity.
