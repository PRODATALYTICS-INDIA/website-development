Fraud Detection & Financial Risk Analytics

Real-time risk signals for suspicious activity, anomalies, and credit decisions.

Challenge

Financial institutions must catch fraud and credit risk quickly without drowning analysts in false positives. Static rules miss emerging patterns and slow down legitimate customers.

Approach

  • Designed AI-powered fraud detection and risk scoring models for transaction and customer signals.
  • Combined anomaly detection with supervised risk scores for suspicious activity prioritization.
  • Supported creditworthiness assessment with interpretable features for decision teams.
  • Architected for near-real-time decisioning where operational latency mattered.

Outcome

  • Stronger detection of suspicious and anomalous activity.
  • Better prioritization for investigators and risk teams.
  • Support for faster, more consistent credit and fraud decisions.
  • A production-ready scoring pattern that risk and ops teams can operate together.
